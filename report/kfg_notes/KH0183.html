Note by Vladimir Muravev:

Rows alternate strictly: a filled row of 11 pendants (white, later brown) and a blank row of 11 in light blue, later yellow-green, which keeps the two W:BB marker cords in places 3–4. The blank rows look like a second column of the account, made from the same template and never filled. The habit appears in Berlin khipus from Ica and Nazca (UR231, UR1176, UR1166, AS128, UR1109, UR1032) and hardly elsewhere.
