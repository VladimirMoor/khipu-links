Note by Vladimir Muravev:

Census form. Nine of the six-pendant groups (each with its own blank top cord) follow the household form of the Santa census khipu UR087 (Medrano & Urton 2018): the first cord holds 1 and the fourth is the largest, here 9 in eleven of sixteen six-cord groups (the Aschers already noted that 9 and 1 dominate this khipu). The group p78–p83 sums these nine households column by column, as the Aschers found (Databook II, 1988): 9, 0, 11, 74, 0, 13 against 9, 0, 11, 79, 0, 13, and its subsidiary 9 equals the sum of the households' subsidiaries. Read as a census, this is a community of nine tributary households with a fixed quota in the fourth column. Among 37 khipus with at least five complete six-cord groups only UR087, UR198, this khipu and Locke's B-8703 (AMNH) have this form.
