Note by Vladimir Muravev:

Probably not from Santa, although the Temple Radicati numbers 1–6 are the Santa census khipus: this khipu is knotted in the SSZ manner (simple and long knots S, figure-eights Z), while the Santa khipus are ZZZ (5 of 5), and its place spacing is shorter. SSZ is a southern-coast habit in the corpus (Arica 4 of 4, Nazca 3 of 8, Ica 7 of 29; none at Pachacamac or Inkawasi).
