Note by Vladimir Muravev:

Its 16 four-cord rows are followed, row by row, by the four-cord rows of UR268 (KH0505), 4.45 times larger on the median — the ratio of the fixed deductions 208 / 47. Its three-cord rows [gross, 47, net] (1653 = 47 + 1606, 1737 = 47 + 1690, 1825 = 47 + 1780, …) are the place-1 records of the rounds of UR269 (KH0506).

Group heads: in 100% of its groups the first cord is the largest (gross first), as in the other detail ledgers of the storehouse, while the summary khipus UR278 and UR270 do not show this rule.

Rows of four cords do not always balance: gross − (deduction + parts) is 11, 34, 10, 10, 2002, 12, 0, 0 — a recurring remainder of about 10 that we could not explain.
