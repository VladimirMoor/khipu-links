Note by Vladimir Muravev:

Probably the same object as UR068 (KH0305, Puruchuco Museum), recorded by Carol Mackey: aligned with UR068, 47 of 65 values and 57 of 61 colours agree (8, 1, 3, 1, 56, 5, 4, 1, 1213, 43, 64, 17 …), and Urton & Brezine's note on UR068 says it 'matches Mackey's #2'. The cord lengths here are all 45.0 (not measured). Real copies in the corpus usually change colours, so this is more likely a second recording than the third copy of the UR064 ~ UR068 account.
