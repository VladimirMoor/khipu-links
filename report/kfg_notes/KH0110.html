Note by Vladimir Muravev:

Many white pendants have a short coloured top (4–9 cm, then white). The colour of that top predicts the value: G0 and BB tags carry 1 (or 0), BG, VB and 0D tags carry 5 (or 0), and cords 14–47 alternate 5 / 1 in pairs (colour explains 94% of the variance of log values, 11% when shuffled).

Details, tests and baselines: Khipu Links Atlas (this khipu), code and data https://github.com/VladimirMoor/khipu-links. Contact: aderby3d@gmail.com.
