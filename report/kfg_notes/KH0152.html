Note by Vladimir Muravev:

A nine-cord row form shared by khipus from four places: the first pendant is the largest, the second small (0.05–0.15 of the first), the third and fourth marked by another colour. It occurs on UR1136, UR1140 (Nazca, Berlin VA16635–6), UR223 and UR194 (Ica), KH0698, HP030 (Pachacamac) and AS038 (Lima), and on only 6 of 280 nine-cord rows elsewhere; the same columns are large and small on all of them, though the hands differ (Z and S knots).

Part of the rows carry the same form written from the other end (pendants 6–7 marked, the eighth small, the ninth largest): UR1136 has it after its forward rows, UR1140 before them. UR194 (Ica) copies a reversed row of UR1140 (group 5) in forward order, so the form was read from either end. The two Berlin khipus, with neighbouring museum numbers, are filled with different numbers and are not linked by sums.
