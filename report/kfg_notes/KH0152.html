Note by Vladimir Muravev:

A nine-cord row form shared by khipus from four places: the first pendant is the largest, the second small (0.05–0.15 of the first), the third and fourth marked by another colour. It occurs on UR1136, UR1140 (Nazca, Berlin VA16635–6), UR223 (Ica), KH0698, HP030 (Pachacamac) and AS038 (Lima), and on only 6 of 280 nine-cord rows elsewhere; the hands differ (Z and S knots).

Its second part (rows 12–20) is form B: pendants 6–7 marked, the ninth the largest; the same two-part layout as UR1140 (Berlin VA16636).
