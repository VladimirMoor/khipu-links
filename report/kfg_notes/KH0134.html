Note by Vladimir Muravev:

Four rows of eight cords with totals 146,087, 49,783, 62,850 and 33,455 are divided by the same shares, about 17.6 / 0.7 / 29.3 / 1.3 / 13.2 / 3.8 / 4.5 / 29.7 % (rows 1 and 2 proportional within 1–2%: 25,691 / 8,731 = 2.94, 43,372 / 14,701 = 2.95). This proportionality is Ascher & Ascher's example 7.5 (Code of the Quipu, 1981) and is discussed by Chirinos (2010). A search of the whole corpus for proportional groups finds only this khipu, AS143 (KH0159) and the Inkawasi nine-part tables; the eight shares do not combine into those of AS143, and the row totals occur on no other digitized khipu.
