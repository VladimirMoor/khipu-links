Note by Vladimir Muravev:

Rows of nine pendants on the nine-cord form of UR1136, UR1140, UR223, HP030 and others, written from the other end: pendants 6–7 in two colours, the eighth small, the ninth the largest in 9 of 10 rows (not a sum of the others). Rows alternate dark and white base colour.
