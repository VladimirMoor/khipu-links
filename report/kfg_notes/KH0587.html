Note by Vladimir Muravev:

Rows of nine pendants on form B (pendants 6–7 in two colours, the ninth the largest in 9 of 10 rows), the form of the second parts of UR1136 and UR1140 (Nazca); the ninth is not a sum of the others. Rows alternate dark and white base colour.
