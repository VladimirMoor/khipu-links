Note by Vladimir Muravev:

Census form. Its six-pendant groups have the household form of the Santa census khipu UR087 (Medrano & Urton 2018): first cord 1 in 0.89 of groups, fourth cord the largest in all; its four-pendant groups [1, x, large, small] are a shorter version of the same form, and the 12- and 18-cord groups are two or three such households in a row. This khipu is not one of the six Santa khipus (Temple Radicati); it came through Gaffron (VA63039, 'Santa Clara, Nazca'), so the form was used beyond Santa.
