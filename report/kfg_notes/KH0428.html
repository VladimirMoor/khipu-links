Note by Vladimir Muravev:

Groups 15–20 repeat the same slots with equal shares of round amounts, the remainder spread by ones: 100 as 33, 33, 34; 140 as 46, 47, 47; 70 as 23, 23, 24. Corpus-wide, runs of cords that differ by exactly one and add up to a round number not divisible by their count are three times commoner than chance (24 against 8.3), and UR190 holds 8 of them.
