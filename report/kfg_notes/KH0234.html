Note by Vladimir Muravev:

The five old untied knots noted by Ascher (p5 1S, p9 2L, p13 2L, p14 1E, p15 3L) were, in four cases, the units knot of a two-cluster cord, so that 12, 12, 11 and 13 became exactly 10; on p5 the tens knot was removed (14 → 4). This looks like a deliberate change of the record (dropping the units) rather than damage; the group sums before or after the change do not give p1 = 30 or p2 = 13.
