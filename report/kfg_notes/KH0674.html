Findings that use this khipu (Khipu Links Atlas)
  • Two Dumbarton Oaks khipus transcribed: UR297 (438 pendants, 623 cords) and UR298 (203 pendants, 285 cords), given by Bill Conklin, exist in the OKR only as scans of Urton's handwritten sheets. We transcribed all 65 pages.
Readings to check
  • Cord p29s1: Value 54, Alt_Value 504. The widest gap between its knot clusters is 1.77 times the usual gap between neighbouring places on this khipu, about two place steps, as an empty place in between would give; this supports the Alt_Value 504.
  • Broken above the units zone: p379 (20), p412 (30). These pendants end in a break (B) shorter than the usual height of the units knots on this khipu (13.5 cm), so their units knots are lost and the values are lower bounds. Over the corpus 92% of such cords end in 0, against 27% of intact ones; the rule holds for subsidiaries too.
