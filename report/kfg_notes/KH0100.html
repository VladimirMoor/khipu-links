Note by Vladimir Muravev:

Provenance 'Peru' could be narrowed: Berlin VA G3304 sits in the G series next to G3297 (UR1088, 'Huacho u. Pachacamac'), G3299 (UR1095) and G3300 (UR1096, both Pachacamac). Like UR1095 it is non-decimal (half of its cords with more than one long-knot cluster or simple knots below a long knot; 15 long knots of 10+ turns), the manner of the Lima coast khipus (Pachacamac, Ancon, Chuquitanta). Worth checking in the museum's acquisition records.
