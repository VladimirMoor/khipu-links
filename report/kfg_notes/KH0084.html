Note by Vladimir Muravev:

A form marked out in colour and filled only here and there: almost all cords are blank, yet each place of a group carries its own short colour segment at the top (W, BS:DB, W:HB, HB, then W ...; in later groups all BS, DB or W), and the few values (2, 3, 4; 8, 4 ...) fall into a few of these columns. Compare the partly filled blank forms KT004 and UR1121.
