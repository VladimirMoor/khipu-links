Links to other khipus
  • UR275 (KH0514) — copy: Shared deposits with deduction 47 (Urton & Chu 2015). The pairing was published before (Urton & Chu 2015); what is added here is the record-by-record comparison.
  • UR278 (KH0517) — totals only: Nine-cord groups of UR278 add up to the gross deliveries of UR266, in order (10 equal and 3 one knot apart in the first 18 groups).
Findings that use this khipu (Khipu Links Atlas)
  • Inkawasi UR269: five supply streams, each with its own deduction: In blocks of five records [gross, net, deduction] the deduction is 47, 46, 46, 44, 25 by place, whatever the deposit size. The place-1 records are deliveries of the stream recorded in full on UR275 (deduction 47 throughout) and its copy UR266: 5 of 10 place-1 records reappear on UR275, at one fixed offset (block b of UR269 = entry b + 15 of UR275), while places 2–5 are found on no other khipu.
  • Inkawasi: empty slots, totals-only copies, product labels: On UR255 the net pendant is empty in the first 12 deposits and filled with the values of UR267A in 5 of the last 6. The bundle UR273A + UR273B records only the deposit totals of UR274A + UR274B: 4 and 6 shared totals (0.3 and 0.5 expected), in the same order on both.
  • Inkawasi: how the storehouse kept its books: The 52 khipus from the Inka storehouse at Inkawasi (Cañete valley) read as one bookkeeping system on three levels. Tables split each delivery into nine parts (UR278, UR270); their group totals are the gross deliveries entered, in the same order, in the ledgers (UR275 and its copy UR266; UR274B).
