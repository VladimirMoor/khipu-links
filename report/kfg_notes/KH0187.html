Findings that use this khipu (Khipu Links Atlas)
  • A 14-unit frame in Gretzer's Berlin batch: Fourteen main groups are rare (1.6% of khipus outside the Berlin 'Pachacamac' batch) but occur on 4 of 23 khipus of that batch: UR212, AS131, AS170 and UR199. The Berlin catalogue describes one more that is not digitized, VA 42537 (14 × 9); two other records with this frame, VA 42513 (14 × 13 pendants) and VA 42532 (7 × A, 7 × B, 7 × A, 7 × B), are UR212 and UR199 themselves, as the KFG release of October 2026 numbers them.
