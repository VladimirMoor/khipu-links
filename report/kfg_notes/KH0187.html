Note by Vladimir Muravev:

The short colour segments at the top of the cords (above all knots) are labels of columns: in the seven-cord groups the tops G0, MB and 0D stand on the 4th, 5th and 6th cords in 11 of 12 groups, whatever the values (67, 33, 133; 150, 50, 50; 0, 0, 0 ...), and never on cords 1–3 or 7. Over the corpus such coloured tops are usually not tied to a place in the group (a band of darkening along the main cord would look the same); here they are.

Findings that use this khipu (Khipu Links Atlas)

The Berlin catalogue describes one more that is not digitized, VA 42537 (14 × 9); two other records with this frame, VA 42513 (14 × 13 pendants) and VA 42532 (7 × A, 7 × B, 7 × A, 7 × B), are UR212 and UR199 themselves, as the KFG release of October 2026 numbers them.
