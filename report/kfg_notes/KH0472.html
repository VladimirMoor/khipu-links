Note by Vladimir Muravev:

Place reading: the OKR gives 505, 109, 203, 207 … where the KFG 2026 file gives 55, 19, 23, 27 (22 of 22). Cords 1–3 put hundreds, tens and units at 6.5, 15 and 28 cm; the disputed cords have their upper knots at 7.5 cm and units at 20–22 cm, a gap of about 13.5 cm, the tens-to-units distance: the whole cord is simply knotted higher, and the KFG reading is supported.

A nine-cord row form shared by khipus from four places: the first pendant is the largest, the second small (0.05–0.15 of the first), the third and fourth marked by another colour. It occurs on UR1136, UR1140 (Nazca, Berlin VA16635–6), UR223 and UR194 (Ica), UR085 (Museo Temple Radicati), UR235 (Berlin VA66830), KH0698, HP030 (Pachacamac) and AS038 (Lima); the same columns are large and small on all of them, though the hands differ (Z and S knots).
