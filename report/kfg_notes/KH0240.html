Note by Vladimir Muravev:

Its few non-cotton pendants are blank markers: nine of human hair (colour LK) and seven of wool (AB) are knotless in 12 of 16 cases, and they open groups 14, 23, 37, 51 (hair) and 39, 41, 45 (wool). In this khipu the largest entry of a group tends to come last, often on a white or green cord (the Leymebamba rule, the mirror of the Inkawasi heads).
