  • A private khipu holds the section totals of Berlin AS109 (Ica): KH0697 (AK002), a khipu in a private collection, formerly in the Maiman Collection in Tel Aviv, without a recorded provenance, has groups that equal the totals of whole sections of AS109, a Berlin khipu from Ica of Gretzer's 1907 batch. Its groups 7 and 8, [755, 1501, 1100, 330, 1136], equal the sum of AS109 groups 33–41 in all five positions; the sum of groups 12–20 differs by one knot (1126); group 4, [766, 1572, 1120, 520, 1137], matches the sum of groups 2–10 in three positions and has 120 moved between the last two (640 + 1017 = 520 + 1137).
