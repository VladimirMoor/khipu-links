Note by Vladimir Muravev:

Urton's (2001) count is reproduced: 24 large groups of 20–22 pendants, each followed by a marker pendant with 8–9 subsidiaries; pendants plus marker subsidiaries give 29–31 per part, 729 in all (730 in Urton 2001). The parts behave as rows of one table (fixed columns, p = 0.001), neighbouring rows are alike, row totals fall in the second half, and from row 9 the rows gain a 22nd column; the markers come in a near-regular A A S S cycle, the large ones after fuller rows.

Details, tests and baselines: Khipu Links Atlas (this khipu), code and data https://github.com/VladimirMoor/khipu-links. Contact: aderby3d@gmail.com.
