Note by Vladimir Muravev:

A nine-cord row form shared by khipus from four places: the first pendant is the largest, the second small (0.05–0.15 of the first), the third and fourth marked by another colour. It occurs on UR1136, UR1140 (Nazca, Berlin VA16635–6), UR223 and UR194 (Ica), UR085 (Museo Temple Radicati), UR235 (Berlin VA66830), KH0698, HP030 (Pachacamac) and AS038 (Lima); the same columns are large and small on all of them, though the hands differ (Z and S knots).

Provenance hint: apart from HP030 (Pachacamac), every khipu with a recorded place that uses this nine-cord form comes from the south coast (Ica, Nazca, 'Costa Sur'); KH0697, also formerly Maiman, holds section totals of the Berlin Ica khipu AS109, so both may come from the Ica–Nazca region.

UR085 and KH0698 are the closest pair on this form: the same colour layout (W W W:X X W W W W W, X = GG on UR085, PB on KH0698), the same place spacing (9 cm), S-dominant knots and nearly the same column proportions (Spearman 0.93 over columns 3–9), at about ten times the scale on UR085; neither sums the other.
