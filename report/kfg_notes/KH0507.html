  • Folded copies: In two independent cases a separate cord in one copy is a subsidiary in the other: UR270 → UR278 at Inkawasi and UR053C → UR053B in the Waterfall set. The packing rule is ours; the near-duplication of B and C was noted by Chirinos.
  • Inkawasi: how the storehouse kept its books: The 52 khipus from the Inka storehouse at Inkawasi (Cañete valley) read as one bookkeeping system on three levels. Tables split each delivery into nine parts (UR278, UR270); their group totals are the gross deliveries entered, in the same order, in the ledgers (UR275 and its copy UR266; UR274B).
