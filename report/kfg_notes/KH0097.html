Note by Vladimir Muravev:

It repeats a part of itself (12, 100, 40, 0, 22, 32, 0 at cords 124 and 135), and so does its copy HP055 (KH0586).

It is longer than HP055: cords 1 to about 185 follow the whole of HP055 (189 pendants; HP055's primary cord ends in a knot, so nothing of it is lost), and cords 181–319 (about 135 pendants, all verso) are not on HP055 but continue with ten or so further records of the same form (a large number of 950–1600, then 10–14, 100 or 200, and small values 6–45). The knots change hand too: simple knots are Z on cords 1–140 (as on HP055) and S from about cord 145 to the end, the switch falling on a group boundary, and the median gap between places drops there from 8.5 to 6.5 cm. Reading: the Paris khipu is the Lima storehouse record copied and then continued, by a second knotter, with later entries.

Lost cords can be restored from HP055. The cords Ascher listed as missing (existence implied by colour and spacing) at p31, p43, p56, p92, p127 and p138 all fall where every record of HP055 has 222, and broken stubs nearby (e.g. p29, p30, value 0 on 6–7 cm) where HP055 has 100 and 40. On UR1084 these positions are dark brown (MB, BY), on HP055 white: the copy lost them to the dark dye, which is known to weaken the fibre.
