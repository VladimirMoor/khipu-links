Note by Vladimir Muravev:

It repeats a part of itself (12, 100, 40, 0, 22, 32, 0 at cords 124 and 135), and so does its copy HP055 (KH0586).

It is longer than HP055: cords 1 to about 185 follow the whole of HP055 (189 pendants; HP055's primary cord ends in a knot, so nothing of it is lost), and cords 181–319 (about 135 pendants, all verso) are not on HP055 but continue with ten or so further records of the same form (a large number of 950–1600, then 10–14, 100 or 200, and small values 6–45). The knots change hand too: simple knots are Z on cords 1–140 (as on HP055) and S from about cord 150 to the end. Reading: the Paris khipu is the Lima storehouse record copied and then continued, by a second knotter, with later entries.
