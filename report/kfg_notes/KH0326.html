Place reading: the OKR gives 105, 102, 202, 302, 401 … where the KFG 2026 file gives 15, 12, 22, 32, 41 (51 of 53 such cords). On this khipu the single knots of two-cluster cords all stand about 8 cm from the main cord and the long knots 16–20 cm, a gap like that between tens and units on other khipus, so the KFG reading (no empty tens place) is the likely one.
