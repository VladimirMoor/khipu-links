Note by Vladimir Muravev:

Two knotters. Cords 1–37 (with their subsidiaries) are tied all in Z, with units at 20–22 cm and few hundreds (values up to 132); cords 38–118 are all S (figure-eights stay Z), with hundreds at 1–4 cm and units at 11–14 cm (values up to 2388). The switch falls between p37 and p38, one cord before the end of the group 34–38, after the largest gap of the first half (10.5 cm). Such switches fall on group boundaries in most khipus where they occur (5 of 8 on the KFG records), and the height of the places changes with the hand, as here. No other khipu in the corpus repeats either part.
