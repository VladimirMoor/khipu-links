A register of the links between surviving Inka khipus

Every link we found between khipus is a copy of one account

We ran arithmetic detectors over about 700 digitized khipus and compared every hit with a baseline that keeps the khipus' structure. What survives is a set of copies and excerpts. Some keep only the totals of an account, some only its details, some split one account over two khipus or join two accounts on one. We found no summary khipu built from other surviving khipus.

        About

        This atlas collects the results of a corpus-wide search for arithmetic links between khipus in the open data of the Open Khipu Repository and the Khipu Field Guide, with two khipus we transcribed from record-sheet scans. Every detector was first run on relations already published, and every result was compared with structure-preserving baselines.

        Data and code

        Scripts, the full report and the study log are in the repository github.com/VladimirMoor/khipu-links. Cord data come from the OKR (MIT licence) and the KFG; please cite them when you use the values.

        How this was made

        The analysis and this site were produced with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, Anthropic), directed and checked by the author. Transcriptions made from scans were checked against the recorders' own totals.
